Social Lab

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Who we are

Social Lab is a marketing agency based on the Gold Coast, Australia. This page explains what we do with the information you give us on our website and landing pages. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you fill in one of our forms, we collect:

  • your name, mobile number and email address
  • your business type, business or agency name, and core suburb or area
  • your answers to our quick questions, such as last year's GCI, properties secured, team size or revenue range
  • which page and which ad brought you to us, from the campaign tags in the link

We also keep your IP address and browser details, mainly to block spam.

How we use it

  • to contact you about our services, including a short call from our team to see if The Ecosystem is a fit
  • to understand which ads and pages are working, so we can improve them
  • to keep our forms and website secure

We may also use advertising tools from Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google to measure results. They may use cookies or similar technology on our pages.

Who sees it

Your details go to our team by email and are stored with our website host. We use service providers to host the site and send email. We don't sell your information. We share it only where needed to run our business, where the law requires it, or with the advertising platforms described above.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need to follow up and manage our relationship with you, then delete or de-identify them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you. Call us on 0459 224 408 or message @sociallabau on Instagram. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, tell us and we'll look into it. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
