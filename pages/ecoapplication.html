Quick questions.

What is The Ecosystem?

Our all-in-one marketing program. We create your content, post it and run all your ads, then report on what's working. No more juggling platforms and partners.

What's included?

Four connected services: content creation (from concept to filming and editing), social media management (captions, scheduling and every detail handled), ad campaigns, and reporting on what's working and where to improve.

What kind of content do you make?

A mix of on-location storytelling and in-studio talking-head content, planned around four pillars: educational content that positions you as the expert, property-specific content like listings and sold stories, personality content that builds trust, and community content that anchors you to your area.

Do you run the ads as well?

Yes. Reel boosting and page growth campaigns put your best content in front of the right people. For agents, we also run seller-lead campaigns in your exclusive core suburbs, and buyer campaigns for your listings using the vendor's advertising budget.

Do I need to be on camera?

Most of the content is built around you, because personality and trust are what win business in your area. We plan it with you, help with ideas and dialogue, and handle the filming and editing.

Who is it for?

Real estate agents and agencies, buyers agents, builders and developers, and property-adjacent businesses like finance and mortgage brokers. The mix of content and ads is different for each, and Emily will point you to what fits.

Can I start smaller?

Yes. There's a beginner content-only package for building momentum before stepping into the full Ecosystem, plus a few monthly levels. Emily will talk you through the right starting point.

What does it cost?

It depends on your business, your area and how much you want us to handle. Emily will talk it through on the call, once we know where you're at.

What happens after I fill in the form?

Emily from our team will be in touch shortly for a quick 5 minute chat to see if The Ecosystem is a fit for your business. There's no obligation.

Who is Social Lab?

A Gold Coast marketing team founded in 2017. We started with property photography and videography, and today our crew covers photography, video, design and content creation, partnering with the Gold Coast's leading real estate and buyers agents.
